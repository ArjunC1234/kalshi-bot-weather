# MaxTemp Engine

This engine predicts the final NWS-recorded daily maximum temperature.

## Belongs Here

- Temperature feature builders.
- Final max-temperature models.
- Quantile/distribution construction.
- Conversion from temperature distributions to Kalshi bracket probabilities.
- Model evaluation helpers specific to weather accuracy.

## Does Not Belong Here

- Trading decisions.
- PnL calculations.
- Order placement.
- Market-price-trained strategy models.
